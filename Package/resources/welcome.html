Arco

Play Apple Music on your Roon zones, from your Mac's menu bar

This installs two things:

  • Arco, in your Applications folder — the menu bar app
  • The Arco audio output — a virtual output the Music app plays to; Arco hands that music to Roon

Your Mac's sound is gone for a moment at the end of the installation, while macOS loads the new output

After installing: in Roon, open Settings › Extensions and press Enable next to Arco
