A test build for UPnP and OpenHome renderers, Linn in particular.

**UPnP and OpenHome**
- A renderer that also offers a plain UPnP AV twin next to its OpenHome source (Linn: "Music Room" and "Music Room:UPnP AV") is shown once, as the OpenHome source
- OpenHome: the next track goes into the renderer's playlist once it has sound, not minutes ahead — a Linn fetched it at once, waited on an empty stream and stopped at the change
- OpenHome: when the Music app moves on a few seconds before a track's announced end, the renderer now simply goes on to the next track (a Linn waited for the rest and stayed in "buffering")
- A renderer that asks for more at the end of a finished track is told "that was all" (a Linn asked again every 70 ms)
- A renderer that stays in "transitioning" at the end of a track is now taken as stopped, so the next track starts
