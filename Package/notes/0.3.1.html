The sample rate in the menu bar, and a few smaller things.

  • The sample rate in the menu bar, next to Arco's icon while it sends to Roon — 44.1, 96, 192 — so you see the clock per track without opening the menu (Show sample rate in menu bar turns it off)
  • After a start or an update, the menu now says Connecting to your Core while Arco reconnects to Roon, instead of briefly showing the Get started list as if something needed approving again. The list only comes back when Roon really asks for Enable
  • More in the log (Show Log): what Roon shows on your zone next to the title Arco sent it, and every reading of a track's sample rate — handy when something doesn't match
