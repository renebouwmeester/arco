A test build for UPnP and OpenHome renderers, Linn in particular.

**OpenHome**
- WAV no longer gets stuck at the end of a track. When a track was complete, Arco held back its last 0.4 s and never closed the connection, so the Linn waited in "Buffering" for the rest until Next was pressed. FLAC was not affected — which is why your FLAC run of the same playlist went through. Thank you for that comparison, it pointed straight at it
- Play in the Linn app after a Pause in the Linn app now starts the Music app again

**Not changed**
- Next and Previous in the Linn app: the Linn only gets the next track once there is sound for it, so at Next it has nothing to go to and stops. Please skip in the Music app
