Arco 1.0. Everything from the 0.5.2 test builds, and a thorough review of the code — thank you to everyone who sent a log

**Roon**
- Arco no longer pauses Roon when the Music app is slow to answer a question — it waits for the Music app instead of taking silence for a stop
- A track whose lossless version arrives just after the start no longer leaves Roon silent for the rest of the track
- A very long hi-res track (an hour or more at 192 kHz) no longer makes Arco quit
- Sound that stops reaching Arco is now started again until it works, and said in the menu when it can't — before, Roon could stay silent while the menu said playing
- After a pause, no fragment of the music from before the pause slips into the stream (this needs the new Arco audio driver, which this package installs)
- Turning Arco off or picking another zone while Roon is still starting no longer leaves a session behind or turns the new one off
- Arco remembers the Core it found, asks it directly, and looks through every network connection of the Mac — as the Roon app does

**UPnP and OpenHome**
- Repeat one, or the same song twice in a row, now plays the second time too
- A streamed track that comes without a length now plays to its end
- WAV no longer gets stuck at the end of a track, and after a change of sample rate the next track arrives in time
- A skip while paused sets the right sample rate
- A renderer with an OpenHome source and a plain UPnP AV twin is shown once

**Not supported**
- Next, Previous and Play in the Linn app: please skip, pause and play in the Music app

Please send your log with any report (menu › Show Log)
