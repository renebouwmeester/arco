Steadier, quicker, and clearer about what it plays.

- **The rate Roon receives**, in the menu: "Playing on KEF LS60 · 96 kHz"
- **Starting**: the switch goes on in one click; Arco waits until the Music app has settled on the track's own rate (it starts at the old one and switches a moment later) — no second start in Roon
- **Pause** reaches the device at once again
- **Next and previous**: quick, also when a streamed track arrives without a length; Roon's previous starts the track over
- **Long albums at 192 kHz**: a stream that would run full ends between two tracks, not in the middle of one
- **A Roon error** gets one more try before Arco switches off
- **Show Log** in the menu opens Arco's log in Finder, for a bug report
- A new icon
- **Apple Music only**: Spotify is no longer a source
