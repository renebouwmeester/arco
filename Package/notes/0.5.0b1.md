Beta — for testers. Besides Roon, Arco can now play Apple Music straight to a streamer on your network, or clock a DAC on your Mac.

**UPnP (Experimental)** — Arco sends the Music app to a UPnP or OpenHome streamer, without Roon:
- One stream per track, each with its own title, artist, album, cover and exact length — your streamer shows them, with a true progress bar
- Gapless: the next track is handed to the streamer ahead (UPnP's SetNext, or OpenHome's own playlist), at its own rate — 44.1 up to 192 kHz, bit-perfect
- Tested on KEF LS60, Meridian Ellipse, Naim Mu-so 2, WiiM Mini, and OpenHome (upmpdcli). Other streamers: please tell us how it goes
- Arco writes one line per streamer in its log with what it supports (SetNext, WAV, FLAC, OpenHome)
- Tips: set the streamer's volume to fixed, keep the Music app at 100, and prefer a wired streamer for 96 and 192 kHz

**This Mac (Experimental)** — the Music app plays to a DAC on this Mac, and Arco only sets the DAC's rate for each track: from the Music app's label, before the first note, never in the middle of a track. No Roon, no Arco driver needed

**Also**
- The main switch is now called Enable; the menu says where the music goes (Roon, Local or UPnP) and the bit depth next to the rate
- Roon is optional in the checklist when you play to a streamer or this Mac
- A pause by the Music app itself (another device took your Apple Music stream, AirPods off) stops writing at once
- After a streamer went to standby during a pause, playing again starts it fresh
