A further refinement of the clock per track: the Music app turns out to name its next track in its log, minutes ahead, and Arco now listens for it.

  • A change of rate is now taken from the format the Music app loads for the very track that comes next. A pause, a seek or a resume of the track that plays no longer counts as the next track's rate — the cause of a seam a few seconds into a track
  • Whether a change of track was natural or a skip is now the Music app's own word, not Arco's estimate of the time left. After a seek in the Music app, the next natural change no longer becomes a fresh start in Roon
  • The format of the next track, loaded ahead, no longer corrects the clock of the track that plays
  • A streamed track that arrives without its length gets the length the Music app loaded
  • In the menu, "Show sample rate in menu bar" has a row of its own; next to "Check for Updates…" it was cut off
