The first public beta of Arco: Apple Music on your Roon zones, from your Mac's menu bar.

What it does

  • Plays what the Music app plays on any Roon zone you pick in the menu — the Roon app doesn't need to be open
  • A clock per track: each track reaches Roon at its own sample rate (with Lossless on in Music), and the menu shows the rate Roon receives
  • Gapless between tracks at the same rate
  • Title, artist, album and cover of each track in Roon; Roon's play, pause, next and previous work, and so does the zone's volume, in Roon or in Arco's menu

Installing

Open the package: it puts Arco in Applications and its audio driver in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/HAL (an administrator password; the Mac's sound restarts for a moment). Arco opens in the menu bar with a short Get started list — the microphone permission (Arco reads its own audio output, nothing else), control of Music, and Enable in Roon's Settings › Extensions (once for each Mac — the extension is called Arco on your Mac's name).

Good to know

  • Roon plays a few seconds behind the Music app, and next / previous take a few seconds to be heard: Roon buffers a live source as it comes in
  • A change of sample rate between two tracks is a fresh start in Roon
  • Roon's progress bar counts through a stretch of tracks at the same rate
  • Hi-res needs a steady connection from Roon to the endpoint
  • Updates arrive through the app (Check for Updates…); Show Log opens the log for a bug report; Uninstall Arco… removes it all

Tested so far on macOS 27 with Apple silicon — reports from other versions and Intel Macs are very welcome.
