A test build for UPnP and OpenHome renderers, Linn in particular. Everything from 0.5.2b6, plus:

**UPnP and OpenHome**
- A skip while paused now sets the right sample rate when you press Play. Knockin' on Your Screen Door (96 kHz) played at 44.1 kHz in your log: the change of rate waited for the resume, and for UPnP renderers it never came

**From 0.5.2b6**
- WAV no longer gets stuck at the end of a track — your Linn waited in "Buffering" until Next
- Play in the Linn app after a Pause in the Linn app starts the Music app again
- Next and Previous in the Linn app are not supported: please skip in the Music app

**Worth trying**
1. Pause, skip to a track at another sample rate (44.1 → 96 kHz), then Play
2. The playlist that got stuck, in WAV
3. Pause in the Linn app, then Play in the Linn app
