A test build. Everything from 0.5.2b8, plus:

**Finding Roon and UPnP renderers**
- Arco now looks for your Roon Core and your UPnP renderers through every network connection of the Mac, as Roon itself does. Before, it asked only through the Mac's default connection — with Wi-Fi, a VPN or a virtual network adapter ahead of the Ethernet, the question never reached the Core, and the menu stayed on "Looking for Roon…" while the Roon app on the same Mac found it at once

**Worth trying**
1. A Mac with more than one network connection (Ethernet and Wi-Fi, or a VPN): does Arco find your Core?
2. Everything from 0.5.2b8: changes of sample rate in WAV, a long pause in the Music app
