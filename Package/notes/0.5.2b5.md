**OpenHome**

Your log showed it six times, every time under a second — thank you for it

**Worth trying**
1. Ten or more tracks in a row, same sample rate, FLAC and WAV — this is where it got stuck
2. A change of sample rate between tracks (44.1 → 96 kHz and back)
3. A skip in the Music app, and Next in the Linn app
4. Pause and Play, in the Music app and in the Linn app
5. A skip while paused
