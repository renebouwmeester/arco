Arco 1.1 beta 3. Everything from beta 2, and one fix

**New in beta 3**
- Turning Arco on while another app plays to the same renderer no longer hangs the menu with a beach ball: the check whether macOS lets Arco talk to the Music app (new in beta 2) could wait forever. It now gets half a second

**New in beta 2**
- Arco no longer hangs when the Music app or Spotify doesn't answer — for example while macOS waits to ask for automation permission after an update. Before, Roon could play its buffer empty and the menu wouldn't open
- The first moments of a track that need a change of sample rate are converted with the highest-quality algorithm: flat to 21 kHz instead of rolling off above 19 kHz
- The hidden FLAC test setting for UPnP renderers is gone; slices are WAV, as they always were by default

**Spotify on a UPnP or OpenHome renderer** (by lzyLuke — thank you)
- A natural change goes on by itself: the next track is handed to the renderer as soon as Spotify starts it, instead of a stop and a fresh start (0.5–3 s of silence before)
- Repeat one plays again, and is gapless from the second loop on
- A seek or a wrong start position no longer leaves the renderer silent until the next track
- Pause and play on a renderer that stopped in between resumes where Spotify is, not from the start of the old track
- A track keeps its identity when Spotify spells its title differently during a pause

**UPnP and OpenHome, any source**
- A slice that fills before its next is known holds back its last second, so a late next still plays without a gap
- After a skip during a pause, the renderer waits for the new track instead of playing a few seconds of the old one
- A change of source during a pause (Spotify while the Music app is paused) plays the new source by itself
- An error on a renderer names the renderer, not Roon

**Roon and the Music app**
- The cover of a streaming track keeps being asked for up to half a minute, instead of the grey placeholder
- A failed write ends the track at what it has; a session without a track no longer stays on "Starting…"; a dropped Roon connection while switching to a renderer no longer turns the bridge off; a quick second click gives the paused source back

**Known**
- A seek inside a Spotify track isn't seen yet: the next change can be a stop and a fresh start (about 5 s)
