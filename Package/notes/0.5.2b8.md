A test build for UPnP and OpenHome renderers, Linn in particular. Everything from 0.5.2b7, plus:

**OpenHome**
- After a change of sample rate the next track reaches the Linn in time. The sound for it comes a few seconds after the change, and Arco looked only once, after a second — so the Linn got it only when the track after it began, and sat on the previous one meanwhile. Now Arco keeps looking until it has gone in

**The Music app**
- Arco double-checks with the Music app when it seems to have missed something: sound while it thinks the Music app is paused, or a track it wasn't told about. In your log the Music app played for an hour without telling Arco, and the Linn never got the next track

**Worth trying**
1. A playlist with changes of sample rate (44.1 ↔ 96 kHz), in WAV
2. A long pause in the Music app, then Play
3. Resume from somewhere other than the Music app window — a media key, AirPods or Control Center

Please send your log with any report (menu › Show Log)
