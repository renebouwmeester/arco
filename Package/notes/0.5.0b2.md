Beta 2 — for testers. A new menu, Sonos, and Roon now truly optional.

**A new menu**
- Three outputs side by side, each with its own switch: Local (this Mac's DACs), Roon and UPnP. Switch off what you don't use and it disappears from the menu
- Roon is off on a fresh install and stays on for everyone who enabled Arco in Roon before. Without Roon, Arco doesn't look for a Core at all: no more "Looking for Roon…"
- Larger text for the status line and for what plays, settings as switches, and the actions (Check for Updates, Show Log, Uninstall, Quit) as menu rows of their own
- Multi-Output and Aggregate Devices are no longer offered as a Local output: Arco can't set their clock per track

**Sonos**
- Sonos speakers now show up under UPnP by their room name. A stereo pair shows once
- Sonos plays 48 kHz at most: Arco converts 88.2, 96, 176.4 and 192 kHz down to 44.1 or 48 for Sonos only, by a whole factor. Every other streamer stays bit-perfect
- Tested on a Sonos Era 100 pair: gapless per track, also across a change of rate

**UPnP**
- A streamer that stops at a new track (a format it can't play) now says so in the menu instead of going silent
- Streamers that need longer to start (TRANSITIONING) get up to 30 seconds
- Confirmed by testers: GentooPlayer with gmediarender, and with mpd/upmpdcli
