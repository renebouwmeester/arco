A refinement of the clock per track.

- The Music app sometimes starts a streamed track in AAC for a quick start, and switches to the lossless stream a moment later. Arco no longer takes the clock from that quick start: it waits for the lossless format, and if that comes only later, it corrects the clock then — and remembers the track's rate for the next time
- After a skip to a track the Music app hadn't loaded ahead, Arco no longer assumes it has the same rate as the one before; it asks the Music app, and the lossless format corrects it if needed
- A pause no longer leaves a trace that could be taken for the next track's rate
- When the Music app names a track's real rate only just after the track began, Arco starts the track over at that rate, instead of changing it a few seconds in
- Arco's icon in Roon's signal path is now a rose sign that fills its circle, instead of a speck, readable in light and dark mode
