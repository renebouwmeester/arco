A refinement of the clock per track.

- The Music app sometimes starts a streamed track in AAC for a quick start, and switches to the lossless stream a moment later. Arco no longer takes the clock from that quick start: it waits for the lossless format, and if that comes only later, it corrects the clock then — and remembers the track's rate for the next time
- Arco's icon in Roon's signal path is now a white sign that fills its circle, instead of a speck
