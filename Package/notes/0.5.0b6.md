Beta 6 — for testers. Pausing and skipping through Roon.

**Roon**
- Pause, skip to another track, play: Roon no longer plays a few seconds of the track you skipped away from before the new one starts
- A track you skip to while paused now gets its own rate when you press play — before, it could play at the rate of the track before it

**The log**
- The transition line counts a skip during a pause as a fresh start, and says when the endpoint took it

Please send your log with any report (menu › Show Log)
