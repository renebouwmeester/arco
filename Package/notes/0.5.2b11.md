A test build. Everything from 0.5.2b10, with seven fixes from a review of the code.

**Fixed**
- Arco no longer keeps using processor time after it is switched off, or after a change of zone while it plays
- A second session on a Linn goes on to the next track again at the end of each track
- The hint "Can't find Roon" now also appears when Arco never found a Core, and when the Core found before doesn't answer
- When another app uses Arco's stream port, Arco takes another one instead of failing to switch on
- Starting to play can no longer begin two sessions on one zone

**Still there**
- Arco remembers the Core it found and asks it directly
- WAV no longer gets stuck at the end of a track
- A skip while paused sets the right sample rate
