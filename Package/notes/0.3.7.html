A fixed 96 kHz for a zone, for a continuous stream into Roon.

  • New under the chosen zone in the menu: "Fixed 96 kHz (no rate switches)". The Arco device stays at 96 kHz for the Music app, so Roon never sees a change of rate: tracks of 44.1, 96 and 192 kHz go on in one stream, with no new start in Roon and no DAC locking on to a new rate at each change. The Music app resamples what isn't 96 kHz, so it isn't bit-perfect — "Follow each track (bit-perfect)" stays the default. Remembered per zone
  • A skip or a new album still starts a new stream, so Roon follows at once
