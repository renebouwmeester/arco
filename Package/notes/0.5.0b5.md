Beta 5 — for testers. A change of rate through Roon on a slower Mac, and a clearer log.

**Roon**
- When the next track has another rate, the Music app pauses for a moment while Arco changes the clock. On some Macs the clock takes a second or more to follow, and Arco then passed that pause on to Roon — Roon could stop on it and leave a long silence before the next track. That pause now stays with Arco, however long the clock takes
- Should Roon still stop just before the change, the next track starts at once instead of after a wait

**The log**
- The transition line says when the change was, and when the endpoint took it
- At a natural change on time it says so, instead of a number that belonged to the next track
- The line about Roon's progress only when something moved
