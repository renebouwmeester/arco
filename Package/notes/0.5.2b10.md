A test build. Everything from 0.5.2b9, with two things from 0.5.2b6 and 0.5.2b8 taken back out, and a sturdier way to find Roon.

**Finding Roon**
- Arco remembers the Core it found and asks it directly next time — and when nothing answers the search at all, it connects to that Core as the Roon app does with the Core it knows
- The search is written in the log: which connections Arco asks through, and every answer that comes back

**Back to the proven**
- Play in the Linn app no longer starts the Music app. After a Pause in the Linn app, the Linn begins the track again from the start while the Music app goes on where it was — the two drifted tens of seconds apart, and that is what made the Linn keep playing after a pause in the Music app, or play another track than the Music app. Please pause and play in the Music app
- The double-check with the Music app from 0.5.2b8 is gone: it went off at ordinary pauses and found nothing missed

**Still there**
- WAV no longer gets stuck at the end of a track
- After a change of sample rate the next track reaches the Linn in time
- A skip while paused sets the right sample rate
