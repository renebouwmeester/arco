The first release of Arco

Apple Music and Spotify on your Roon zones, from the menu bar.

  • Any Roon zone from the menu, with the Roon app closed
  • A clock per track: each track at its own sample rate (Music, with Lossless on)
  • Gapless between tracks at the same rate
  • Title, artist, album and cover in Roon; Roon's buttons and the zone's volume work
  • Music and Spotify, one at a time — the one that plays leads
  • A Get started list, Open at login, Uninstall Arco…, and updates through the app
