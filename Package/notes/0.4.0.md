The clock per track, told by the Music app itself — and a log that says how each change of track went.

- The Music app names each track it loads with a label, and every format it sets up for that label carries the rate and whether it is lossless (AAC has a fixed run-in, lossless none). Arco now reads that label first: the rate of this very track, no guessing from the moment a line was written. A skip or a start waits up to 3 s for it; a natural change has it a minute ahead
- An AAC quick start is recognised by its label and never sets the clock
- No change of clock in the middle of a track any more: when the Music app only knows the right rate after the first seconds, the track plays on, resampled, and the rate is remembered for the next time. A correction in the first seconds still goes back to the track's start, before Roon plays it
- The bit depth next to the rate in the menu bar — "24/96" — from the same label, for every track
- A warning in the menu when the Music app's volume isn't 100: only at 100 does it play bit-perfect. "Set to 100" fixes it
- Arco's log now has one line per change of track: "seamless", or what was heard and why. Send the log with a bug report (Show Log in the menu)
