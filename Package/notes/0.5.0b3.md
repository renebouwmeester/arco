Beta 3 — for testers. The clock on time on macOS 15 Sequoia, and a clearer log.

**macOS 15 Sequoia**
- The Music app on Sequoia doesn't write the lines Arco uses on macOS 27 to know the next track's rate ahead. Arco now reads the one Sequoia does write when it loads the next track, and sets the clock at the change itself instead of a few seconds into the track
- Announcing the next track ahead to a UPnP streamer still needs its title, which Sequoia doesn't give: between tracks of different rates a streamer may still stop briefly

**Also**
- After the Music app pauses around a change of clock, Arco asks it for six seconds whether it plays again — it doesn't always say so itself
- The transition meter: at a change of rate it speaks for Arco's side only; after a stop, the next track counts as a start

**The log**
- When the Music app's log has no answer for a change of track, Arco now writes what it held at that moment
